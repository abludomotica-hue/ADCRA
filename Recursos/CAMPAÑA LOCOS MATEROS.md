CAMPAÑA LOCOS MATEROS.

URL del Cliente: https://www.locosmateros.cl/

Create a cohesive cinematic advertising film for the Chilean yerba mate brand "Locos Materos".

CAMPAIGN CONCEPT:
"¿Dónde estás tú? Está tu mate."

The film tells a warm, authentic and emotionally engaging story about how mate accompanies everyday life in Chile. The mate is not presented merely as a product; it is a symbol of connection, friendship, family, work, study, neighborhood life and shared moments.

The story begins at dawn and follows different Chilean people throughout their day, eventually bringing them together around mate.

VISUAL IDENTITY:
Premium cinematic lifestyle advertising combined with authentic Chilean documentary photography.

Use the provided reference image as the main visual inspiration for:
- warm earthy color palette
- natural golden light
- intimate atmosphere
- editorial photography
- realistic mate preparation
- wooden and natural textures
- sophisticated but approachable visual language
- soft contrast
- subtle cinematic film grain

COLOR PALETTE:
Earthy greens, yerba-mate greens, warm beige, natural wood, dark brown, cream and charcoal.

LIGHTING:
Natural sunlight whenever possible.
Warm early-morning sunlight.
Golden-hour tones.
Soft shadows.
Realistic practical lighting indoors.
Avoid artificial studio lighting.

CAMERA:
Cinematic 35mm and 50mm photographic look.
Use 24mm for environmental establishing shots.
Use 85mm and macro-style close-ups for mate, yerba and hands.
Shallow depth of field for emotional close-ups.
Natural handheld movement mixed with slow cinematic dolly and tracking shots.
Movement should feel human and elegant, never robotic.

VISUAL STORY:
The film progresses from solitude to community.

Beginning:
One person preparing mate at home.

Middle:
Different Chilean people carrying mate through everyday life:
home, neighborhood, work and university.

Ending:
Friends and family sharing mate together.

CHARACTERS:
Authentic Chilean people between approximately 20 and 45 years old.
Natural Chilean appearance and contemporary everyday clothing.
Include diversity in age, gender and appearance.
Avoid stereotypical folkloric costumes.

LOCATIONS:
Authentic contemporary Chilean environments, especially Santiago:
- Chilean home
- kitchen
- residential neighborhood
- Santiago streets
- workplace
- university
- cozy home gathering

Do not make the locations look like tourist advertisements.

MATE:
The mate must remain physically consistent and realistic throughout the film.
Traditional mate gourd.
Realistic yerba mate leaves.
Realistic stainless-steel bombilla.
Realistic thermos.
Correct proportions.
Correct placement of yerba and bombilla.
Natural steam and hot water.

IMPORTANT PRODUCT RULE:
Never generate or invent the Locos Materos logo.
Never distort brand logos.
The real Locos Materos logo will be added during post-production.

PERFORMANCE:
People must behave naturally.
No exaggerated commercial smiles.
No unnatural poses.
No looking directly into the camera unless explicitly requested.
Natural gestures, conversations, laughter and interaction.

EDITING FEEL:
The individual clips should feel as if they were filmed by the same cinematographer, with the same camera system, lenses, lighting philosophy and production design.

The film should gradually increase in emotional energy:
quiet morning → daily movement → university/work → connection → celebration.

FINAL EMOTION:
Warmth, belonging, friendship, Chilean identity and the simple pleasure of sharing mate.

The audience should finish the film feeling:
"Donde esté, mi mate me acompaña. Y cuando hay mate, hay gente."

FORMAT:
Vertical 9:16.
Photorealistic.
High-end commercial production.
Natural motion.
Cinematic composition.
Realistic human anatomy.
Realistic hands.
Realistic facial expressions.
No text generated inside the video.
No subtitles generated inside the video.
No artificial-looking CGI.
No fantasy elements.
No exaggerated slow motion.
